🔒 Privacy & Safety

Your security matters — here’s how we protect you.

⭐ Overview

The Meme Encyclopedia is designed to be a fun, safe, and secure place to explore internet culture. We do not collect sensitive information, we do not store passwords, and we do not access your Google account.

📧 Why We Require Gmail Login

To keep the Meme Encyclopedia safe for everyone, we require users to sign in using their Gmail account. This helps prevent anonymous abuse, spam, scams, and harmful behavior. When you log in, we only receive your Gmail address and a unique user ID — nothing else from your Google account.

Your Gmail is used strictly for safety, moderation, and account recovery. If a user ever posts something dangerous or illegal, the Gmail login allows us to identify the account responsible and cooperate with proper authorities if needed. This protects our community and ensures that everyone can enjoy the website without worrying about anonymous harm.

Your Gmail is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. It exists only to keep the site safe, friendly, and fun.

🔐 Firebase Authentication Safety

Our login system is powered by Google Firebase Authentication, a trusted and secure industry‑standard service used by millions of websites.

Firebase ensures:

  • ✔ Your password is never visible to us
  • ✔ Your Google account is never accessible to us
  • ✔ All login data is encrypted
  • ✔ We only receive your email and a unique user ID

Even if we wanted to see your password — we literally cannot. Firebase handles everything on Google’s side.

📄 What Data We Store

We only store the information needed for your profile and site features:

  • • Email (for login)
  • • Username
  • • Profile picture
  • • Bio
  • • Coins
  • • Friends list
  • • Posts you create

We do not store:

  • ✘ Passwords
  • ✘ Google login tokens
  • ✘ Contacts
  • ✘ Location data
  • ✘ Private Gmail information

🛡️ How Your Data Is Protected

All data stored in Firebase Firestore is protected by:

  • ✔ Google‑level encryption
  • ✔ Secure HTTPS connections
  • ✔ Authentication rules preventing unauthorized access

Only you can access your account. Not even the site owner can view your private info.

🚫 We Do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your data
  • ❌ Share your data with third parties
  • ❌ Track you outside this website
  • ❌ Store sensitive information
  • ❌ Access your Google account

💬 Questions or Concerns?

If you ever have questions about safety, privacy, or how your data is used, feel free to reach out or check this page anytime.
