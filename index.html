📜 Classic Meme Timeline (2005–2014) 📜

2005 — Pepe the Frog
Pepe first appeared in Matt Furie’s comic *Boy’s Club*.
Not a meme yet — just “feels good man.”
2006–2007 — LOLcats / I Can Has Cheezburger?
Early cat memes circulated in 2006.
The big boom happened in 2007 with *I Can Has Cheezburger?*
2007 — Rickroll & Keyboard Cat
Rickroll becomes a massive prank trend.
Keyboard Cat goes viral after being uploaded in 2007.
2008 — Trollface / Rage Comics Begin
Trollface posted on DeviantArt in 2008.
Rage comics explode: Rage Guy, Forever Alone, Me Gusta.
2006–2009 — Advice Animals
Advice Dog appears in 2006.
Format explodes in 2009: Philosoraptor, Courage Wolf, SAP, Foul Bachelor Frog.
2010 — Rage Comics Peak
Rage comics dominate the internet.
Forever Alone & Me Gusta become iconic.
2011 — Nyan Cat
Uploaded April 2011.
One of the most iconic looping memes ever.
2012 — Bad Luck Brian

The king of unlucky memes.
2013 — Doge
