⭐ Classic Meme Timeline (2005–2014) ⭐

2005 — Pepe the Frog
Pepe first appeared in Matt Furie’s comic Boy’s Club (Issue #1).
Not a meme yet — just a frog saying “feels good man.”
Pepe wouldn’t become a meme until a few years later.
2006–2007 — LOLcats / I Can Has Cheezburger?
Early “Caturday” cat images circulated on SomethingAwful and 4chan around 2006.
The big boom happened in 2007 when I Can Has Cheezburger? launched.
This era created the “funny cat + broken English” meme style.
2007 — Rickroll & Keyboard Cat
Rickroll became a massive prank trend using Rick Astley’s Never Gonna Give You Up.
Keyboard Cat, filmed in the 1980s, became a meme after being uploaded to YouTube in 2007.
2008 — Trollface / Rage Comics Begin
Carlos Ramirez posted Trollface on DeviantArt in 2008.
This kicked off the rage comic era: Rage Guy, Forever Alone, Me Gusta, etc.
2006–2009 — Advice Animals
The first Advice Animal, Advice Dog, appeared in 2006.
The format exploded in 2009 with Philosoraptor, Courage Wolf,
Socially Awkward Penguin, Foul Bachelor Frog, and more.
2010 — Rage Comics Peak
Rage comics dominated this year.
Forever Alone symbolized awkward loneliness.
Me Gusta symbolized weird satisfaction.
2011 — Nyan Cat
Created by Chris Torres and uploaded in April 2011.
Instantly became one of the most iconic looping memes ever.
2012 — Bad Luck Brian
Kyle Craven’s school photo became the legendary unlucky meme.
One of the most recognizable memes of the decade.
2013 — Doge
Kabosu’s 2010 photo exploded into a meme in late 2013.
Comic Sans captions like “much wow” and “very doge” defined the style.
2014 — This Is Fine Dog
KC Green’s Gunshow comic was published in 2013,
but the “This Is Fine” panel became a meme in 2014, symbolizing calm disaster.
