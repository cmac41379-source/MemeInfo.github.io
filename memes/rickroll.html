📜 Rickroll Origin

The Rickroll meme began in 2007 when users were tricked into clicking a link that led to Rick Astley’s 1987 hit “Never Gonna Give You Up.” The bait‑and‑switch prank spread across forums and social media, becoming one of the most recognizable internet jokes ever. Its charm lies in the absurdity and persistence — no matter how many times you’ve been Rickrolled, it’s still funny.
