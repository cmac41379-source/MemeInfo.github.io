🍔 Cheems

The Bonk Dog

📜 Origin

Cheems is a Shiba Inu known for his adorable, round face and his love of “cheemsburgers.” The meme originated from a photo of a dog named Balltze from Hong Kong.

🔥 Meme Impact

Cheems became famous for the “S” speech pattern — adding extra letters to words like:
“cheemsburger,” “sneep,” “frens,” “bonk,” “shibe.”

He appears in Doge lore memes, Swole Doge vs Cheems, and wholesome meme formats.

💡 Fun Facts

  • Cheems’ real name is Balltze.
  • He became popular around 2019.
  • He is part of the Doge extended universe.
  • He is known for “bonk” memes.
