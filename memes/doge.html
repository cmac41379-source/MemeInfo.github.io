📜 About Doge

Doge is one of the most iconic memes of all time, originating from a picture of a Shiba Inu named Kabosu. The meme became famous for its colorful Comic Sans captions like “wow”, “such meme”, “very doge”.

📅 First Appearance

2010 — and it exploded in popularity around 2013.
