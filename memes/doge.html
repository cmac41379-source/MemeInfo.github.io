Doge

Origin: 2010

Doge originated in 2010 when a Japanese kindergarten teacher posted photos of her Shiba Inu, Kabosu, online. The dog’s quirky, side‑eye expression quickly spread across forums like Reddit and Tumblr. By 2013, the image evolved into the iconic meme style using colorful Comic Sans text expressing exaggerated inner thoughts (“much wow,” “very doge”). It became popular because the expression was funny, relatable, and easy to remix, turning Doge into one of the most recognizable internet memes ever.
